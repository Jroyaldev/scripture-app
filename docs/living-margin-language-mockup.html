Form

Clause Parallel gift triad, v. 8 · NA28
Force

Presents prophecies as something that will eventually cease or be rendered inoperative—not merely “fade.”

Consequence

Establishes an eventual contrast between present partial gifts and the coming completeness (vv. 9–12).

Do not overstate

Morphology alone does not identify when cessation happens, or resolve the cessationist–continuationist debate.
