Every source now carries a real mark. Three are approved and ship the publisher's own reversed variant. Three more were gathered and reversed here — one ink on unaltered artwork — because those publishers release only a dark-on-light logo. One had no usable mark at all — its cover art sets the logotype in three stacked lines, so it is relaid horizontally here as a reconstruction to show what we would ask for. Records for the six unapproved sources are illustrative: placeholders to judge the design, not catalogue entries.

The perspective line

The lightest mark on the card: a dot and two words, no border, no case change, sitting under the byline. Every phrase is one the source uses of itself, so it reads as an imprint's own description rather than our verdict on it. A reader outside that camp gets the signal without being told what to think.
