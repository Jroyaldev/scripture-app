# C0.1 — Traces View Redesign (Visual Lab)

> **Status:** Complete (2026-07-18)
> **Component:** `lab/` Traces projection only
> **Current maturity:** L1 experimental routing dialect
> **Target maturity:** L2 coherent, responsive visual prototype

## Objective

Rebuild the Pattern Shapes lab's Traces mode as a calm, source-linked study
surface. A trace must stay attached to exact words, remain understandable at a
glance, and scale from one focused relationship to several held relationships
without turning Scripture into a wiring diagram.

The durable interaction stays: the page is quiet at rest, touching words wakes
their relationship, clicking holds it, and the inspector returns to every source
member. The implementation should feel textual and editorial rather than like a
generic graph or electrical schematic.

Annotation lines are the primary language, not a dispensable decoration. Traces
keeps them attached to exact words and routed through deterministic gutter lanes;
the passage score supports annotation, comparison, and navigation without
replacing those lines.

## Acceptance

- The mode switch describes user intent (`Reading | Traces`), not renderer jargon.
- Every focused trace activates its exact marked phrases, and every member row is
  a real source-return action; wrapped phrases retain a truthful anchor.
- One trace owns attention. Additional held traces stay compact and switchable
  without permanent edge soup.
- The focused annotation owns the nearest gutter lane. At most three quiet held
  comparisons remain lined beside it; additional held annotations remain explicit
  and switchable in the score (`N held · 4 lined`).
- The rail is a real passage score: a bounded path/evidence projection, active member
  state, source-return actions, note, export, and an inviting empty state.
- Kind is legible without color alone through line rhythm or terminal shape.
- Every binary kind has a deliberate silhouette at three rendered-line distances:
  same line, one line apart, and many lines apart. The lab exposes all 18 cases
  together through the reversible `Angle proof` fixture.
- Every angle-proof case lives inside one synthetic text row and one reference.
  Its first phrase sits in the middle of a rendered line and its second phrase
  ends at the right edge; the measured line delta is exactly 0, 1, or 6.
- Contact dots share the underline centerline and overlap its end by a controlled
  amount; the connector begins at that same contact, with no detached elbow.
- Different-line connectors leave each contact vertically into the measured
  interline corridor, travel through whitespace, and make their large rounded
  turns on a shared true-margin rail. They do not cross prose or verse numbers.
- Same-line cradles stay inside the measured leading below their source line,
  reserving clearance for the next rendered line.
- Multi-member spines stop at their first and last rounded ports. Parallel
  shoulders leave both actual rails and converge at the phrase, without a center
  spur or a rule extending beyond the turn. Binary connectors and threads use
  the same margin origin. Same-line members inside a longer thread share one
  rounded port and fork only after reaching their common corridor.
- Wrapped selections retain all rendered fragments. The nearest-margin fragment
  owns the connector and remaining fragments keep quieter continuation terminals.
- On shared words the focused relationship owns the wash and the underline
  nearest its contact dot; held relationships remain as quieter stripes above it.
- Paper, Ink, Glass, and Candlelight preserve the same hierarchy.
- Wide desktop, 1024px, and 640px layouts remain usable with no horizontal
  overflow; the rail becomes an in-flow surface on narrow screens.
- Reduced-motion users receive the completed geometry without draw choreography.
- Existing authoring, local prototype persistence, member editing, undo, notes,
  Revelation matrix, and export remain functional.
- A reversible 14-trace Psalm 1 fixture proves the design under overlapping,
  same-line, nearby, long-distance, and multi-member density.
- A separate Revelation 2–3 fixture renders all 51 verses without truncation and
  grows the score from 4 to 22 traces (92 additional exact-phrase source anchors).
  First, middle, and last tracks remain reachable in a bounded score.

## Non-goals

- No integration into `src/renderer/**` or the live Electron app.
- No durable Substrate format, migration, or autonomous write path.
- No solution to translation-fragile phrase anchoring; it remains the next
  engineering gate in `lab/TODO.md`.
- No infinite canvas, force-directed global graph, semantic zoom, marks library,
  or new relationship kinds in this pass.
- No changes to canonical `lab/patterns.js` or `lab/verses.js` fixtures; reversible
  stress records stay local to the visual harness.
