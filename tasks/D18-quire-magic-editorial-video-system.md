# D18 — Quire Magic Editorial Video System

## Objective

Promote the isolated `/magic` Tour Lab from a sequence of individually polished
semantic cards into a consistent editorial-video system that a low-cost Luna
director can drive across a complete podcast-shaped tour. Use the existing saved
director corpus first, including at least 20 records, and treat the house—not the
model—as the owner of continuity, shot cadence, component variation, entrances,
exits, and visual repetition control.

## Required outcome

- Audit at least 20 saved director records and the newest full Luna tour as one
  evidence corpus. Measure visual intervals, opening/closing dead air, retained
  and rejected kinds, repeated kinds, scene/beat balance, focus usage, and
  component succession rather than judging isolated screenshots alone.
- Preserve policy-3 grounding, canonical Scripture resolution, precise refusal,
  Luna-medium/OpenRouter admission, one current visual composition, exclusive
  focus, append-only evidence, and all existing D17 migrations.
- Keep Luna's job semantic and cheap: identify grounded teaching actions, cues,
  passage references, and concise claims. Do not ask it to design layout,
  animation, duration, shot continuity, or a unique page for every moment.
- Add a broad but finite library of repeatable house-owned video components and
  variants. Components must be semantically mapped, compositionally coherent,
  and capable of producing meaningful variety without random decoration.
- Add deterministic editorial sequencing over a full tour: opening movement,
  sustained rhythm, chapter/step transitions, visual rests, callbacks, variation,
  and a closing movement. Prevent long accidental emptiness and monotonous runs
  of the same component while preserving honest silence when no claim is grounded.
- Separate semantic events from rendered shots so one accepted action may expand
  into a short house-authored shot sequence without inventing theology or text.
  Every shot must retain traceability to its originating scene/beat/evidence.
- Prefer full-podcast or multi-step replay acceptance over one short clip. Reuse
  existing Luna evidence. The original one-call calibration restraint was
  explicitly superseded by the human's instruction to iterate quickly with cheap
  Luna calls; every added call must still answer a named full-podcast quality gap
  and remain separately costed.
- Add deterministic metrics, fixtures, tests, browser captures, and documentation
  that prove consistency over time as well as premium quality per frame.

## Non-goals

- No search-ranking or retrieval redesign.
- No shipped Listen-room or main-renderer integration.
- No Sol calls or direct-vendor substitutions.
- No autonomous Scripture/Substrate mutation.
- No claim that synthetic house-QA proves broad organic Luna selection.

## Verification target

- Evidence audit covers at least 20 records and names measurable failure modes.
- A multi-step/full-podcast replay exercises the final editorial sequence with no
  provider traffic.
- Browser QA checks temporal spacing, component succession, seek/resize parity,
  one-current-composition, exclusive focus, no overflow, and clean logs at desktop
  and mobile widths.
- Focused and full repository suites pass; STATUS and Tour Lab documentation state
  what is proven, what remains synthetic, and whether the optional call was used.

## Landed 2026-08-03

The isolated Tour Lab now has a house-owned editorial compiler in
`lab/tour-lab/magic-editorial.mjs`. Seven stable components cover bookends,
listening, Scripture, anchors, evidence relations, margins, and focus. A complete
half-open shot list owns openings, movement handoffs and closings, scene arrivals,
semantic phases, bounded callbacks, explicit rests, optional family punctuation,
transitions, and provenance. Play, direct seek, resize, and reduced motion resolve
the same one current composition with at most one short outgoing slot.

when at least 3s exists; passage resolves up to 3.5s; 3–10s callbacks with at most

than two; focus no more than 12.5%; and a 60% hard semantic ceiling. The preferred
40–55% band is not a quota: a movement below 40% validates only when the validator
independently recomputes zero remaining grounded callback opportunity.
