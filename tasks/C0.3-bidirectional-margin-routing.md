# C0.3 — Bidirectional margin routing

> **Status:** Complete 2026-07-18
> **Component:** Loom margin-route generator, scorer, and held-rail projection
> **Current maturity:** L2 left-only routing with dormant side-aware seams
> **Target maturity:** L2 deterministic mirrored left/right routing

## Objective

Make `left` and `right` real, symmetric whole-route choices. The engine must
generate and score a complete legal route on either margin, allocate strands
independently by side, and keep held ticks visually slaved to the side selected
by their shadow plan. The result should choose the calmer viable margin without
flapping on focus changes or weakening any hard clearance rule.

This is the second geometry prerequisite for section-aware handoffs and the
strict TypeScript app port. It does not yet allow one route to switch sides.

## Contract

- The engine derives stable left and right loom data from the full measured
  obstacle set. Hosts may pin them independently with `leftLoomX` and
  `rightLoomX`; legacy `loomX` remains the left-side alias.
- Margin geometry is one mirrored grammar:
  - left pins use the fragment's left edge and pour outward-left;
  - right pins use the fragment's right edge and pour outward-right;
  - level exits, terminal turns, shoulders, swoops, drips, corridor claims,
    spines, and ports reflect exactly across a symmetric fixture.
- A side is legal only when its strand remains inside the corresponding
  measured margin and at least 4px inside the block bounds.
- Strand occupancy is keyed by `(side, strand)`. A committed left strand never
  suppresses the same numbered right strand, and vice versa.
- Candidate generation finds the first legal strand independently for each
  requested side. The scorer compares those complete candidates using actual
  ink length, wrong-side preference, and the existing 12px previous-side
  hysteresis. Equal scores break deterministically by requested side order and
  then strand.
- Unsupported side values are declined, never interpreted as left.
- Existing left-only callers remain pixel-identical when they request only
  `left` or omit `sides`.
- Route-lab and Shapes request both sides. Each host remembers the last
  committed side per annotation and supplies it as `previousSide`.
- A held tick uses its valid shadow plan's chosen side. It remains inside the
  SVG bounds, retains a stable phantom hit target during bloom, and falls back
  deterministically when no legal shadow route exists.
- Shadow candidates remain side-effect free. Only valid painted plans commit
  corridor, spine, and strand claims.

## Acceptance

- A symmetric fixture produces left and right plans whose contacts, segments,
  ports, spine, and X-bounded claims are geometric reflections with equal
  unrounded length and identical exit grammar.
- Right-side level, comb, tag, corridor, and multipoint paths emit finite
  geometry and pass obstacle validation.
- A left `(strand 0)` claim does not block right `(strand 0)`; a right claim on
  the same interval does. Each side escalates only after its inner legal strand
  fails.
- The shorter legal side wins. A prior side remains selected when the competing
  improvement is under 12 score units and changes when the improvement exceeds
  the hysteresis.
- Right-side corridor claims preserve C0.2 sharing: disjoint X spans reuse a Y
  rung; overlapping spans stagger deterministically.
- The route suite contains an explicit right-margin winner and keeps every
  drawn route green in all 15 focus states throughout the 460–760 width sweep.
- Dense Psalm and Long Revelation visibly use both margins when legal; all
  held annotations remain represented by side-correct ticks. No malformed SVG,
  console errors, focus loss, or horizontal overflow at 1280px or 640px.
- Paper, Ink, Glass, and Candlelight retain the one-line vocabulary and calm
  visual hierarchy; right routes do not collide with the passage score.
- `node --check`, direct engine tests, the full test suite, lint, and
  `git diff --check` pass.

## Non-goals

- No side switch within one route, section-gap measurement, plural spines, or
  S-through-section topology.
- No app renderer, persistence, phrase-anchor, event, or Substrate changes.
- No second passage margin or score redesign.
- No new line type, kind glyph, or decorative right-side exception.
