# D17 — Quire Magic Luna Director Policy

## Objective

Promote `/magic` from a scene-shaped, fixed-budget Luna direction experiment to a
versioned semantic-beat director whose richer visual frequency remains quiet on
screen. Luna continues to run as `gpt-5.6-luna-medium` through OpenRouter. Search
and the Sol roster are out of scope.

## Required outcome

- Preserve verbatim transcript cues, canonical Scripture grounding, refusal of
  unsupported claims, one current thought, and exclusive highlights.
- Separate structural passage/context scene ownership from a flat ordered list
  of independently timed semantic visual beats.
- Ask Luna to classify the teacher's action first and choose the most specific
  supported artifact. `group` is the fallback for a genuine within-verse wording
  pattern, not the generic safe answer.
- Give compact positive instruction examples for group, footnote, term,
  allusion, compare, chain, caveat, aside, and highlight.
- Derive scene/movement capacity from clip duration and grounded opportunities;
  a house prelude must not evict a valid Luna scene.
- Measure transient thought cadence separately from structural scene changes.
- Batch all qualified quiet intervals into one fill request and request at most
  one grounded beat for each selected interval.
- Support bounded verse ranges in compare and chain inputs, with deterministic
  house-owned canonical excerpt selection and refusal of invalid or oversized
  ranges.
- Replace global aside exclusion with thought-slot replacement and a short
  aside-to-aside cooldown.
- Keep per-type limits as explicit safety ceilings. Dwell remains renderer-owned
  and evidence-tuned rather than model-authored.
- Include a director-policy version and transcript hash in cache identity.
- Record proposed, precisely rejected, accepted, focus-masked, and actually
  visible artifact outcomes without writing user substrate.
- Version the durable contract, migrate supported local replay/evidence shapes,
  refuse newer unsupported versions, and update Tour Lab documentation.

## Verification

- Focused Node tests prove schema/version/cache behavior, duration-aware policy,
  scene/beat ownership, range resolution/refusal, batched gap selection, precise
  dispositions, thought-only cadence, aside cooldown, and focus masking.
- Every checked-in `/magic` replay validates under the current schema and replay
  playback remains deterministic across seek/rebuild.
- Browser QA proves richer serial beat changes never create more than one
  ordinary current thought, highlights remain exclusive, and the stage does not
  acquire horizontal overflow at representative desktop and mobile widths.
- Existing saved Luna evidence is used before purchasing any new result. If a
  live behavior check is still necessary, it uses only the minimum number of
  `gpt-5.6-luna-medium` OpenRouter calls and records their cost/provenance.

## Non-goals

- No search-ranking, retrieval, Listen-room, or shipped-app integration work.
- No Sol calls.
- No autonomous Scripture/Substrate mutation.
