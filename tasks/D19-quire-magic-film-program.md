# D19 — Quire Magic Film Program

## Objective

Promote `/magic` from a sequence of individually polished semantic shots into a
beautifully edited visual film of an entire podcast program. Luna continues to
supply meaningful, grounded moment selection; the deterministic house system owns
the translation of those choices into an intentional end-to-end program — scenes,
timing, pacing, transitions, rests, callbacks, titles, and layouts. The theater
playback experience is the whole scope; the ask/making/tour-list framing scenes
stay as they are.

## Required outcome

- Judge the complete program, not isolated screenshots: add maintained,
  provider-free whole-program audit and capture tooling that measures and
  photographs every shot across an entire multi-step replay.
- Improve every visual type at the reusable house-component level. One visual
  language, but composition-level differentiation per frame family; no
  card-skeleton uniformity.
- Keep visuals frequent enough to sustain attention without busyness; no
  arbitrary limits, random spacing, mechanical timing, long accidental gaps, or
  rapid visual stacking that the eye can read as metronomic.
- Preserve complete thoughts: never awkwardly truncate titles, notes, captions,
  or semantic copy anywhere in the theater.
- Keep titles, Scripture references, captions, and visual elements intentionally
  aligned on desktop and mobile.
- Give Luna a clear film-direction brief and enough freedom for a rich visual
  program; house code owns consistent layout, pacing, safety, and presentation.
- Land the director-policy bump to `magic-director-4` with declared-policy
  validation: historical policy-3 evidence and replays remain loadable and are
  validated against the frozen v3 rulebook; new writes are policy-4 only.
- Never hand-fix one replay. Improve prompts, compiler rules, timing logic, and
  components so every future Luna run benefits; acceptance is a fresh paid Luna
  run on a contrasting genre, promoted to a named replay.

## Verification

- `program-audit` / `program-capture` baselines committed for the Exodus,
  Romans, and atlas replays before component/timing changes; before/after
  whole-program comparisons committed under `output/`.
- Fresh full-program Luna run (25–40 minute ask, contrasting genre) audited and
  captured as one film; promoted to a named replay with
  `provenance: 'luna-medium-evidence'`. All spend separately recorded.
- Desktop 1280, mobile 390, short-phone 320; light theme; reduced motion;
  seek/resize parity; replay request firewall (zero model-route traffic); clean
  consoles.
- Focused contract/editorial/Scripture suites and the full repository suite
  pass; lint passes.

## Non-goals

- No search-ranking or retrieval redesign. No Sol calls.
- No ask/making/tour-list scene redesign; no Listen-room or main-app port.
- No autonomous Scripture/Substrate mutation.
- No destructive supersession of the D18 Exodus acceptance fixture; policy-3
  evidence stays auditable under its declared policy.
