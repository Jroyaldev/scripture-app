# D16 — Quire Magic editorial precision polish

## Objective

Continue the isolated Tour Lab `/magic` visual-stage refinement after D15. Audit every renderer in the
real replay surface, name the compositions that still look unfinished, and raise the weakest ones to the
same premium editorial standard as the rebuilt loom without increasing simultaneous density.

## User-visible outcome

- Each artifact reads as an intentional editorial composition rather than a decorated data card.
- Typography, line length, optical alignment, negative space, rules, labels, and Scripture attachment are
  precise at desktop and narrow widths.
- The visual family remains varied by meaning while sharing one unmistakable house grammar.
- The page stays quiet: one current thought, restrained gold, no ornamental noise, no accidental boxes,
  and no layout jump beneath stable Scripture.
- The audit names any renderer that remains below the premium bar instead of declaring blanket polish.

## In scope

- `lab/tour-lab/magic.html`, `magic.css`, `magic.js`, and pure visual-contract helpers when required.
- Cost-free `visual-artifact-atlas` and checked-in replay inspection at desktop and mobile viewports.
- Focused lifecycle/layout contracts, screenshot evidence, audit notes, task status, and `STATUS.md`.

## Out of scope

- Search, retrieval, corpus ranking, prompts, provider selection, or paid model calls.
- Changes to the main renderer, Listen room, or unrelated dirty work.
- New visual types or increased concurrent visual density.

## Acceptance

- Every supported renderer is reviewed in-browser and recorded as pass, improved, or still open.
- Weak renderers receive composition-specific fixes rather than one generic card treatment.
- Titles and labels are sentence-case, optically aligned, non-overlapping, and kept close to the visual
  relationship they name.
- Desktop and 390px layouts have no horizontal overflow and remain above the listening rail.
- Playback, seeking, replacement, and highlight exclusivity preserve the D15 one-thought contract.
- Focused contracts, lint, syntax, diff integrity, and the full suite pass with zero provider calls.
