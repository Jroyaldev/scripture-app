# C0.4 — Section-aware plural spines and handoffs

> **Status:** Complete (2026-07-19)
> **Component:** Loom section topology, plural-route contract, and host rendering
> **Starting maturity:** L2 deterministic whole-route left/right selection
> **Delivered maturity:** L2 deterministic section-aware routing with legal S handoffs

## Objective

Let one long semantic trace use the calm margin beside each of its declared
sections and change sides only through measured section whitespace. A legal
change reads as one continuous S-shaped line: tributaries feed a section-owned
spine, the spine pours through a fully clear declared gap, and the next spine
continues on the other margin. The engine must never infer semantic sections
from reflow or turn a text crossing into a scored compromise.

This is the final geometry prerequisite before the strict TypeScript and app
port. It generalizes the route contract now so the app does not bind itself to
the obsolete singular-spine shape.

## Contract

- Hosts declare semantic structure explicitly:
  - each routed anchor carries a stable `sectionId`;
  - rendered lines preserve stable IDs, document order, and section provenance;
  - `block.sections` supplies ordered finite section bounds;
  - `block.sectionGaps` supplies finite measured whitespace between named
    adjacent sections.
- Section routing is opt-in. Missing, duplicate, malformed, out-of-order, or
  undeclared section data never creates a handoff; the existing C0.3 whole-side
  planner remains the safe fallback and records a structured decline.
- A geometric state is `(sectionId, side, strand)`: two sides × three strands,
  or six state cells per active section. Because the final calm-tie rule prefers
  fewer handoffs and is not safe to apply to incomplete prefixes, each cell may
  retain up to `N` labels keyed by handoff count. The dynamic program therefore
  remains polynomial at `O(N² × 36)` transition work; it does not enumerate
  exponentially many complete routes.
- Strand and spine occupancy remains local to `(side, strand, vertical span)`.
  Each emitted run owns explicit plural corridor, spine, and strand claims.
- A state transition is legal only through a declared gap between the two
  semantic sections. The entire left-to-right run and both tangent turns must:
  - remain inside the measured gap;
  - clear every expanded word, number, heading, and additional obstacle;
  - honor deterministic handoff claims from previously committed routes.
  Crossing ink is illegal, never expensive.
- The handoff uses the existing one-line vocabulary as one monotone stretched S
  with vertical tangents at both spines. It always descends in document order
  and has no dead section-rule plateau, new stroke, glyph, rail, or decorative
  exception.
- The complete topology score uses actual ink length, per-section semantic-side
  fit, and 12-unit hysteresis for each semantic section whose side differs from
  the last committed topology. Finite raw costs accumulate in forward document
  order. Only a complete route is rounded to its tenth-pixel score before routes
  compare lexicographically by that score, fewer handoffs, requested side order,
  strand, and stable section order; prefix pruning never applies that
  complete-route tie rule early or rounds atomic contributions.
- The canonical section plan exposes:
  - `sideRuns[]` with stable IDs, ordered section IDs, side, strand, X, and
    vertical extent;
  - `spines[]` owned by a side run;
  - `handoffs[]` owned by the two adjacent runs and the declared gap;
  - role-owned route parts/segments, ports, and plural claim arrays;
  - a deterministic `topologySignature` and bounded-work diagnostics.
- Same-side strand changes are not silently drawn as miniature handoffs. A
  maximal side run keeps one strand; a state change in this gate changes side
  through a declared semantic gap.
- Singular `side`, `strand`, `marginRailX`, `spine`, `spineClaimOut`, and
  `strandClaimOut` remain compatibility aliases only when exactly one margin
  run exists. A mixed plan must not pretend its first run describes the whole
  topology.
- Route Lab and Shapes iterate canonical route parts and every spine for
  drawing, weaving, native validation, and bounds checks. A held tick takes the
  side of the shadow plan's run for that tick's semantic section.
- Only valid painted plans update topology memory or commit any plural claim.
  Shadow plans remain side-effect free.

## Acceptance

- A two-section fixture with opposite semantic bias and a clear declared gap
  selects a two-run left→right or right→left topology when its complete score
  beats both whole-side routes. Its two spines, tangent S handoff, ports, claims,
  ownership metadata, and signature are finite and deterministic.
- Mirroring the fixture reflects every spine, tributary, handoff control point,
  claim, and side assignment exactly while retaining the same route grammar and
  unrounded length.
- Removing, narrowing, misnaming, or obstructing the declared gap makes the
  side transition impossible. The planner chooses a legal whole-side topology
  or returns an honest needs-space result; it never crosses the obstruction.
- Three or more sections support multiple legal handoffs and same-side run
  coalescing without disconnected ink, duplicate spine fragments, port
  overshoot, or false singular aliases.
- Existing annotations without section data and annotations contained in one
  section preserve the C0.3 topology and paint geometry.
- Existing claims independently force a section run outward or invalidate a
  handoff; disjoint vertical runs may still reuse the same side/strand.
- Route Lab includes an explicit visible S fixture and a blocked-gap fixture.
  The rendered query gate repeats all focus states across 460–760px and proves
  deterministic signatures, native SVG validity, exact ownership, hard gap
  clearance, bounds, focus structure, and absence of browser errors.
- Long Revelation uses its declared seven-letter structure. At least one legal
  trace demonstrates a calm section handoff while held ticks remain side-correct
  and the passage score remains untouched at 1280px and 640px in all four
  atmospheres.
- Planner diagnostics prove polynomial `O(N² × 36)` work over the six geometric
  side/strand states and at most `N` handoff-count labels per state, not the
  number of possible complete assignments.
- Focused tests, the full suite, strict TypeScript lint, JavaScript syntax,
  `git diff --check`, and final browser logs pass.

## Non-goals

- No strict TypeScript/core port in this task.
- No reading-canvas renderer integration, passage-score product integration,
  persistence, RevisionStore mutation, or Substrate change.
- No durable phrase-anchor format or translation migration.
- No inferred paragraphing, free mid-line side crossing, diagonal text crossing,
  or handoff without declared measured semantic whitespace.
- No new annotation kind, stroke vocabulary, theme, or manual side selector.
